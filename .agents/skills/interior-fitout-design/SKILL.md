# Interior Fit-Out & Technical Detailing

Technical fit-out execution guide for commercial, retail, hospitality, and high-end residential interior construction.

## 1. Technical Drawing Deliverables (Shop Drawings)
* **Reflected Ceiling Plan (RCP):**
  * Ceiling grid, gypsum board bulkheads, cove lighting details.
  * Coordination of MEP ceiling services: HVAC supply & return linear diffusers, fire sprinkler heads, smoke detectors, emergency lighting, access panels ($600\text{mm} \times 600\text{mm}$).
* **Flooring & Setting-Out Plan:**
  * Datum point / tile starting grid to minimize awkward cut tiles at doorways and visible thresholds.
  * Expansion joints and transition strips between dissimilar floor finishes (e.g., marble to parquet brass schluter profiles).
* **Millwork & Bespoke Joinery Details:**
  * Carcass substrate (MDF, Marine Plywood, Solid Hardwood), edge-banding thickness ($1\text{mm} - 2\text{mm}$ ABS/PVC).
  * Hardware specifications (Blum soft-close concealed hinges, undermount drawer slides, Hafele LED profile channels).

## 2. Partition Systems & Acoustic Ratings (STC)
* **Standard Drywall:** Double-layer $12.5\text{mm}$ gypsum board on each side of $70\text{mm}$ light-gauge steel studs with $50\text{mm}$ mineral wool insulation ($\text{STC} \ge 48 - 52\text{ dB}$).
* **Wet Areas:** Moisture-resistant green board (MR) or cement board (Aquapanel) with polyurethane waterproofing membrane extending $2.1\text{ m}$ high in shower enclosures.
