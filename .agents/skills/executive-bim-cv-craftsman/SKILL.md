# Executive BIM CV Craftsman (تصميم وصياغة السيرة الذاتية الاحترافية لكبار المهندسين)

A career strategy and resume engineering skill tailored for Senior Architects, BIM Managers, Computational Designers, and AEC Software Developers. It authors **100% ATS-compliant** (Applicant Tracking System) resumes and executive portfolio CVs that emphasize measurable business impact, complex technical proficiencies, multi-disciplinary coordination track records, and professional licensures.

## 3. The XYZ Impact Formula for Engineers
Never write passive duties like *"Responsible for drawing Revit plans"*. Always transform them into quantifiable value:
- **Before**: *"Created Revit models and resolved clashes."*
- **After (XYZ Formula)**: *"Resolved 850+ structural-MEP clashes on a 45,000 m² hospital project using Navisworks, preventing an estimated \$180K in field rework and change orders."*
- **Before**: *"Wrote scripts to help the team."*
- **After (XYZ Formula)**: *"Engineered a custom pyRevit extension suite adopted by 40 architects, accelerating batch sheet generation and title block population by 60%."*
