# Arabic Visual Design & Typography

Aesthetic guide for Arabic graphic design, cultural geometry, and typography in architecture and presentation boards.

## 1. Classical & Contemporary Arabic Calligraphy Styles
* **Kufic (الخط الكوفي):**
  * Architectural, geometric, structural.
  * Ideal for: Masonry engraving, stone facades, minimalist modern logos, and bold poster headers.
* **Thuluth (خط الثلث):**
  * Grandiose, ceremonial, intricate ligatures.
  * Ideal for: Formal titles, cultural center entrances, and commemorative plaques.
* **Naskh / Ruq'ah (النسخ والرقعة):**
  * Highly legible, fluid, balanced proportions.
  * Ideal for: Architectural drawing annotations, body paragraphs, and functional labeling.

## 2. Arabic Typography & Font Pairing
* **Geometric Modern:** Clean strokes matching contemporary steel and glass architecture:
  * Primary: **Cairo**, **Almarai**, **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**, **DIN Next Arabic**.
* **Humanist / Traditional:** Warm organic modulation matching stone, brick, and landscape:
  * Primary: **Adobe Arabic**, **Amiri**, **Boutros**, **Frutiger Arabic**.
* **Contrast Rules:**
  * When pairing Arabic with Latin scripts, match optical x-height and stroke weight rather than raw point size (Arabic fonts often render smaller than Latin fonts at the same pt size).

## 3. Cultural Geometry & Ornamentation
* **Girih & Islamic Geometric Patterns (IGP):**
  * 4-fold, 6-fold, 8-fold, and 12-fold star tessellations.
  * Application: Parametric shading screens (Mashrabiya louvers), decorative perforated panels, and water-jet cut stone lobbies.
* **Culturally Grounded Color Palettes:**
  * Desert Earth: Sand Gold (`#D4AF37`), Raw Terracotta (`#C86D51`), Warm Ochre (`#CC8B3C`).
  * Oasis Green & Azure: Deep Emerald (`#097969`), Persian Cobalt (`#1C39BB`), Turquoise (`#30D5C8`).
