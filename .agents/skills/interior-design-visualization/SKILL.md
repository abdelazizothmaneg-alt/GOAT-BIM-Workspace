# Interior Design Visualization

Specialized visual transformation and staging skill for interior designers, architects, and 3D visualizers.

## 1. Style Frameworks & Aesthetics
* **Japandi & Minimalist:** Natural oak/ash woods, low-profile furniture, textured plaster/lime-wash walls, earthy neutral tones (beige, terracotta, warm grey), organic ceramic decor.
* **Modern Luxury / Contemporary:** Large-format marble slabs, brass/bronze metal accents, concealed cove LED lighting, velvet and bouclé upholstery, fluted wood paneling.
* **Biophilic & Scandinavian:** Maximized daylight, blonde woods, indoor living walls/planters, woven textiles, airy open-plan circulation.
* **Industrial Loft:** Exposed brickwork, black steel structural frames, polished concrete floors, vintage leather, Edison/track pendant lighting.

## 2. Structural & Preservation Invariants
* **Geometry Preservation:** Retain load-bearing columns, perimeter walls, ceiling heights, and window/door openings.
* **Perspective & View Alignment:** Maintain realistic eye-level horizon lines ($1.2\text{ m} - 1.5\text{ m}$ above finished floor) or wide-angle architectural framing ($20\text{mm} - 28\text{mm}$ equivalent).
* **Lighting Strategy:** Layer ambient (recessed downlights), task (pendant above kitchen island/desk), and accent lighting (LED strips in millwork, wall sconces).
