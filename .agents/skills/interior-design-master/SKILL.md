# Interior Design Master

Turnkey interior architecture and executive design director framework for high-end luxury residential, hospitality, and corporate environments.

## 1. Executive Design Review Framework
Every space must pass five core audit gates:
1. **Spatial Logic & Flow:** Does the layout maximize natural light, sightlines, and effortless pedestrian circulation?
2. **Structural Feasibility:** Do ceiling drop levels accommodate HVAC fan coil units (FCU), recessed linear diffusers, and plumbing slopes?
3. **Material Authenticity & Longevity:** Are materials rated for their intended traffic class (e.g., high-traffic porcelain tiles vs. sensitive honed marble, high-abrasion upholstery fabrics with Martindale $> 40,000$ rubs)?
4. **Circadian Lighting Integration:** Does the lighting scheme support tuneable white temperature shifting from crisp $4000\text{K}$ midday to restful $2200\text{K}$ evening?
5. **Smart Automation (Home Automation):** Integration of KNX, Lutron, or Crestron lighting, motorized drapery, and hidden audio speakers.
