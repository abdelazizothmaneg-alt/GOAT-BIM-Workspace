# Architectural & Urban Code Validator

Expert regulatory compliance engine to cross-check architectural drawings, urban master plans, and site layouts against zoning ordinances and building codes.

## Compliance Verification Engine

### 1. Zoning & Dimensional Regulations
* **Setbacks (Front, Rear, Side):**
  * Check minimum clear distance from property boundaries to exterior building walls.
  * Validate projection allowances (balconies, cornices, canopies, basement car parks).
* **Maximum Building Height & Angular Envelopes:**
  * Validate maximum allowed building height and story count relative to road widths.
  * Sky exposure planes / angular light envelopes (e.g., $1:1$ or $1:1.5$ setback angle to protect street daylight).
* **Frontage & Active Edge Requirements:**
  * Minimum percentage of transparent glazing on ground floor facades along primary pedestrian streets (typically $\ge 60\%$).
  * Maximum blank wall length (typically $< 6\text{ m}$ continuous).

### 2. Life Safety & Fire Separation
* **Fire Separation Distance:**
  * Minimum distance between facing buildings based on wall fire-resistance ratings and percentage of unprotected openings.
* **Fire Truck Access & Hardstanding:**
  * Minimum road width for emergency vehicles ($\ge 6.0\text{ m}$ clear width, $4.5\text{ m}$ vertical clearance).
  * Turning radius (inside radius $\ge 6.5\text{ m}$, outside $\ge 12.0\text{ m}$) and designated staging zones adjacent to building facades.
* **Egress & Travel Distance:**
  * Maximum common path of travel and exit separation distance (1/3 or 1/2 of overall floor diagonal).

### 3. Accessibility & Universal Design
* **Circulation & Entrances:**
  * Ramp slope limits (maximum $1:12$, landing every $9.0\text{ m}$ run).
  * Accessible parking allocation (e.g., ADA/Local code ratios with minimum $2.4\text{ m}$ width $+ 1.5\text{ m}$ access aisle).
