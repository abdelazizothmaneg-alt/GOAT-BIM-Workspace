# Architecture & Design Firm Lead Generation

Strategic business development playbook to attract, qualify, and win high-margin architectural and interior design commissions.

## 1. High-Value Client Personas
* **High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNWI):** Luxury private residences, penthouses, vacation villas. Focus: Discretion, turnkey craftsmanship, emotional storytelling, bespoke exclusivity.
* **Boutique Real Estate Developers:** Multi-family residential, mixed-use commercial, hospitality. Focus: Net-to-Gross efficiency, construction cost control, sales velocity, visual marketing assets.
* **Corporate & Commercial Tenants:** Office headquarters, retail flagship stores, F&B hospitality. Focus: Brand alignment, employee retention/ergonomics, fast-track delivery, code compliance.

## 2. Lead Qualification Framework (BANT-AEC)
* **Budget:** Does the client have a realistic cost-per-square-meter understanding plus adequate contingency ($15 - 20\%$)?
* **Authority:** Are you speaking directly with the ultimate decision-maker (property owner, CEO, investor)?
* **Need & Scale:** Does the project align with your firm's core portfolio strengths and staff bandwidth?
* **Timeline:** Is the procurement schedule realistic, or will unrealistic deadlines compromise technical quality?

## 3. Proposal Win-Themes & Pitch Architecture
* Differentiate through **Risk Reduction**: Emphasize BIM clash-free coordination, strict cost tracking, and 3D visual approvals before procurement.
* Structure RFP technical bids with clear visual methodologies, organigrams, and proven project case studies highlighting ROI and award-winning delivery.
