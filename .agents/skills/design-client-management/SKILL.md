# Design Client Management

Professional practice guide for managing client relationships, expectations, design milestones, and contractual scope in architecture and interior design projects.

## 1. Client Onboarding & Project Brief Formulation
* **Design Questionnaire:** Capture functional requirements, spatial budget, lifestyle habits, target aesthetic preferences, and non-negotiables.
* **Budget-to-Scope Alignment:** Check proposed client wishlist against regional construction cost benchmarks ($/m^2$ or $/sqft$) before conceptual design begins.
* **Milestone Schedule Sign-off:** Define explicit review gates:
  1. Concept Design (Spatial layout & mood boards).
  2. Schematic Design (3D visualizations & general arrangements).
  3. Design Development (Material selections & lighting schemes).
  4. Construction Documentation (Tender drawings, BOQ, and specs).

## 2. Managing Revisions & Scope Creep
* **Revision Limits:** Standard contract allows up to two major revision rounds per milestone stage.
* **Change Order Protocol:** Any modification requested after milestone sign-off triggers a formal Change Order (CO) specifying:
  * Description of variation.
  * Impact on project delivery timeline (days/weeks).
  * Additional professional fee and revised contract total.
  * Explicit client signature requirement prior to commencement.

## 3. Client Presentation Strategy
* **Storytelling Arc:** Problem context $\rightarrow$ Conceptual narrative $\rightarrow$ Spatial solution $\rightarrow$ 3D visual walkthrough $\rightarrow$ Detailed specifications.
* **Curated Options:** Never overwhelm clients with more than two distinct, well-reasoned design directions (Option A: Primary recommended; Option B: Alternative bold/budget variant).
