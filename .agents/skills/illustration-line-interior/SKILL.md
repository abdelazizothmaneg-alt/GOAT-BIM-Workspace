# Illustration Line Interior

Minimalist, graphic architectural line art and diagrammatic illustration style for interior presentations.

## 1. Visual Stylistic Language
* **Line Weight Hierarchy:**
  * Heavy contour line ($0.5\text{mm} - 0.7\text{mm}$): Outer building boundaries and section cut lines.
  * Medium line ($0.35\text{mm}$): Major furniture silhouettes, door swings, and cabinetry outlines.
  * Fine detail line ($0.18\text{mm} - 0.25\text{mm}$): Floor plank patterns, tile joints, textile folds, and hatching.
* **Axonometric & Isometric Cutaways ($30^\circ / 30^\circ$ angles):**
  * Exploded views showcasing floor finishes, floating furniture arrangements, and ceiling drop levels simultaneously.
* **Palette & Shading:**
  * High-contrast black linework on pure white or subtle warm off-white background.
  * Optional monochromatic accent tones or pastel spot colors (e.g., warm terracotta, sage green, or muted ochre) to highlight specific zones or circulation paths.
