# Competition Board Curator (تنسيق وإخراج لوحات المنافسات المعمارية)

An architectural presentation and graphic curation skill dedicated to structuring winning international and GCC competition presentation boards (ISO A0 and A1 formats). It establishes rigid modular grid systems, architects visual narrative arcs (Hook, Logic, Proof, Detail), balances render-to-drawing ratios, and ensures strict bilingual (Arabic RTL and English LTR) typographic harmony.

---

## 1. Board Anatomy & Visual Narrative Arc

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ACT I: THE HOOK (Top 30-35% of A0 Board)                        │
│  - Project Identity & Title (Bilingual Arabic/English)           │
│  - THE HERO RENDER (Spans 4-6 columns, striking emotional shot)  │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  ACT II: THE LOGIC & STORY (Middle 25-30% of Board)              │
│  - 3-to-4 Step Massing Evolution Diagrams (Form Finding)        │
│  - Environmental & Contextual Analysis (Sun, Wind, Shading)      │
│  - Programmatic & Circulation Stacking Schemes                   │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  ACT III: THE PROOF & TECHNICAL RIGOR (Bottom 30-35% of Board)   │
│  - Scaled Architectural Plans (Ground / Typical Level)           │
│  - Comprehensive Cross-Section & Elevation with Human Scale      │
│  - Material Callouts & Tectonic Construction Detail              │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Modular Grid System (ISO A0: $841 \times 1189\text{ mm}$)

- **Outer Margins**: $20\text{ mm}$ all sides (ensures content is not clipped by physical mounting frames).
- **Column Grids**:
  - **Portrait (Vertical A0)**: 6 equal columns ($120\text{ mm}$ wide each) with $15\text{ mm}$ gutters.
  - **Landscape (Horizontal A0)**: 8 or 12 equal columns with $12\text{ mm}$ gutters.
- **Rule of Whitespace**: At least $20 - 25\%$ of the overall board area must remain clear negative space to let the jury's eye breathe.

## 3. Bilingual Typographic Hierarchy (Arabic & English)

When preparing competition submissions for Middle East entities (e.g., Royal Commission for Riyadh City, Diriyah Gate, NEOM, Red Sea):

| Element | Arabic Font / Weight | English Font / Weight | Recommended Size (at A0) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Project Title** | Cairo / Almarai (Black / Heavy) | Inter / Helvetica Neue (Bold) | $60 - 72\text{ pt}$ |
| **Subheadings** | Cairo (Bold) | Inter (Semi-Bold) | $28 - 36\text{ pt}$ |
| **Concept Statement** | Naskh / Cairo (Medium) | Inter (Regular) | $16 - 18\text{ pt}$ |
| **Technical Body Text** | Almarai (Regular) | Inter (Light/Regular) | $12 - 14\text{ pt}$ |
| **Drawing Scale & North** | DIN Next Arabic / Mono | JetBrains Mono / Arial | $10 - 11\text{ pt}$ |

---

## 4. Key Graphic Rules for Juries
1. **The 5-Second Test**: The jury must instantly grasp the project's unique selling proposition (USP) from 2 meters away via the Hero Image and Concept Diagram before reading any small text.
2. **Aligned Orthogonal Cross-References**: Align plan section cuts vertically with the corresponding cross-section drawing directly below it.
3. **Palette Restraint**: Limit the graphic color palette to a monochromatic background with at most **one or two bold accent colors** (e.g., Emerald Green for landscaping, Terracotta/Orange for public pedestrian flow).
