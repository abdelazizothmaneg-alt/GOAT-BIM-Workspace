# Deep Research & Market Intelligence (Firecrawl Workflows)

Investigative web intelligence engine executing systematic multi-query exploration, web crawling, data aggregation, and synthesis for AEC, urban development, and market proposals.

## 1. Deep Research Execution Workflow

Follow this 4-stage systematic research loop:

```text
[Research Brief] ──> [Query Decomposition] ──> [Deep Crawling & Extraction] ──> [Synthesis & Cross-Check] ──> [Dossier]
```

### Stage 1: Query Decomposition
* Break broad topics into 4 to 8 targeted sub-queries exploring distinct dimensions:
  * Economic indicators, market pricing benchmarks ($/m^2$ or $/sqft$).
  * Municipal planning master plans, regional infrastructure spending.
  * Competitive analysis and case studies.

### Stage 2: Deep Web Crawling & Scraping
* Ingest long-form articles, municipal council PDF reports, and developer prospectus documents.
* Extract quantitative statistics, dates, regulatory amendments, and official executive quotes.

### Stage 3: Cross-Checking & Fact Synthesis
* Reconcile contradictory statistics across sources (e.g., differing population growth forecasts).
* Document source credibility and confidence levels for all cited metrics.

---

## 2. Deliverable Dossier Structure

Output a comprehensive report (saved to `docs/market-research/<topic>.md`):
1. **Executive Briefing:** Bottom-line conclusions and strategic implications.
2. **Key Findings & Data Matrices:** Structured comparison tables with verified historical and projected metrics.
3. **Regulatory & Planning Landscape:** Relevant codes, municipal incentives, and timeline risks.
4. **Strategic Recommendations:** Actionable next steps for proposal bids, client pitches, or design strategies.
5. **Bibliography & Source Registry:** Chronological, full-URL attribution list.
