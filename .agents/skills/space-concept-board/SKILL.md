# Space Concept Board

Creative direction and narrative curation for architectural and interior spatial concept boards.

## 1. Concept Board Structure
A comprehensive concept board consists of four balanced visual zones:
1. **The Hero Mood / Emotional Anchor ($40\%$ area):**
   * High-impact lifestyle or architectural photography setting the overarching atmosphere, lighting mood, and spatial grandeur.
2. **Material Swatches & Textures ($30\%$ area):**
   * Tactile physical finishes: Honed natural stone, raw timber grain, textured bouclé/linen fabric, brushed metal finishes, ribbed fluted glass.
3. **Form & Furniture Vocabulary ($20\%$ area):**
   * Distinct silhouettes representing the design language (e.g., sculptural curved sofas, minimalist cantilevered tables, linear pendant lights).
4. **Color Palette & Chromatic Accents ($10\%$ area):**
   * Swatches of 4 to 5 harmonized colors with Hex/Pantone codes indicating Base (60%), Secondary (30%), and Accent (10%) roles.

## 2. Narrative Development
* Every concept board must articulate a **Design Manifesto**:
  * *Theme Title:* (e.g., "Wabi-Sabi Sanctuary", "Urban Brutalist Warmth", "Mediterranean Solarium").
  * *Sensory Experience:* Description of light diffusion, acoustic intimacy, and tactile interaction.
  * *Design Philosophy:* How the proposed finishes reflect client identity, historical context, or biophilic well-being.
