# Architecture Rendering

Photorealistic architectural exterior visualization and digital rendering art direction.

## 1. Composition Typologies
* **Eye-Level Street Perspective ($1.6\text{ m}$ Camera Height):**
  * Emphasizes the pedestrian human scale, entrance canopy, landscaping, and active ground-floor activation.
* **Low-Angle Hero Shot:**
  * Accentuates verticality, dramatic facade cantilevers, and structural massing against dynamic sky backdrops.
* **Bird's-Eye / Aerial Perspective ($30\text{ m} - 100\text{ m}$ Elevation):**
  * Shows overall master plan layout, road hierarchy, parking, green buffer zones, and surrounding urban context.

## 2. Environmental Lighting & Atmosphere
* **Golden Hour / Warm Sunset:**
  * Low sun angle, long cast shadows, warm $3200\text{K}$ facade wash contrasting with cool sky reflections on glass curtain walls.
* **Overcast Diffuse Lighting:**
  * Uniform soft light, zero harsh shadows, highlighting true material textures (concrete, terracotta panels, timber slats, zinc cladding).
* **Night / Twilight Architectural Lighting:**
  * Deep twilight blue sky, interior warm illumination glowing through glass, exterior landscape uplighting on feature trees and facade accent spotlights.

## 3. Sketch-to-Render Pipeline
* Input: Hand-drawn architectural concept sketches or raw massing geometry from Rhino, SketchUp, or Revit.
* Output: High-fidelity rendered presentation boards matching materiality, fenestration patterns, and lush native foliage.
