# Architectural Review & Critique

Senior architectural peer-review framework to stress-test and evaluate building designs prior to client presentation or planning submission.

## 1. The 6-Point Peer-Review Checklist

### 1. Site Integration & Urban Grain
* How does the massing relate to neighboring street walls, datum lines, and pedestrian sightlines?
* Are public entrances intuitively located and celebrated through architectural hierarchy (canopies, double-height volumes, setbacks)?

### 2. Spatial Efficiency & Net-to-Gross Ratios
* Assess the building efficiency ratio:
  $$\text{Efficiency (\%)} = \left(\frac{\text{Net Usable / Lettable Area (NUA/NLA)}}{\text{Gross Floor Area (GFA)}}\right) \times 100$$
  * Target: Commercial Office $\ge 80 - 85\%$; Residential $\ge 82 - 88\%$; Hotels $\ge 70 - 75\%$.
* Verify core layout (elevators, fire stairs, MEP risers, public restrooms) for optimal travel distances and minimal dead corridors.

### 3. Facade Tectonics & Massing Rhythm
* Balance solid-to-void ratios (typically $40:60$ or $50:50$ for optimal solar heat gain control).
* Assess shadow lines, reveal depths, and sun-shading louvers (brise-soleil) relative to cardinal solar orientations.

### 4. Daylighting & Passive Environmental Design
* Are primary living/working spaces oriented within $15^\circ$ of True South/North for daylight harvesting?
* Is cross-ventilation possible? Are building depths under $14\text{ m}$ for dual-aspect natural lighting?

### 5. Structural & MEP Logic
* Verify column grid regularity ($8.4\text{ m} \times 8.4\text{ m}$ or $9.0\text{ m} \times 9.0\text{ m}$ optimal for car parks and office layouts).
* Check vertical MEP shaft alignment from roof plant rooms to basement pump/electrical rooms.

### 6. Constructability & Lifecycle Maintenance
* Accessibility for facade cleaning (cradle systems), roof drainage slopes, and expansion joint locations.
