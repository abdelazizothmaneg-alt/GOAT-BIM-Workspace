# Architecture Design Master

Executive architectural design framework integrating formal morphology, environmental responsiveness, structural elegance, and human experience.

## 1. Contextual & Bioclimatic Morphological Analysis
* **Macro & Micro Site Reading:**
  * Topographical contours, prevailing seasonal winds (wind roses), solar trajectory angles (sun path diagrams), and urban view corridors.
* **Massing Generation Strategies:**
  * Subtractive carving: Carving courtyards and light wells out of solid mass to induce natural stack ventilation.
  * Additive articulation: Stepping terraces and interlocking volumes responding to human scale and mountain/waterfront horizons.
  * Aerodynamic deflection: Shaping high-rise forms to minimize vortex shedding and ground-level wind turbulence.

## 2. Spatial Programming & Volumetric Flow
* **Program Adjacency Matrices:**
  * Direct physical connection (A), visual connection only (V), acoustic separation (X), indifferent (O).
* **Sectional Architecture & Volumetric Hierarchy:**
  * Design in cross-section: Double-height public gallerias, clerestory daylight scoops, sunken courtyards, and mezzanine reading nooks.
  * Spatial progression: Transitioning from compression (intimate low-ceiling entry vestibules $2.4\text{ m}$) into release (expansive cathedral ceilings $> 6.0\text{ m}$).

## 3. Tectonic Articulation & Material Honesty
* Express structural logic: Exposed post-and-beam timber frames, board-formed fair-face concrete, structural steel trusses, and ventilated terracotta rainscreens.
* Facade depth and solar modulation: External brise-soleil, perforated mashrabiya screens, deep window reveals, and shadow kinetics.
