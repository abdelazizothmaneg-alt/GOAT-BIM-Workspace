# Real Estate Photo Generation

Commercial real estate photography, virtual home staging, and digital marketing presentation engine.

## 1. Virtual Staging & Decluttering
* **Vacant Room Staging:**
  * Populate cold, empty real estate interiors with proportionally accurate, stylish furniture (contemporary Scandinavian or transitional luxury).
  * Automatically ground furniture with realistic contact ambient occlusion shadows on floor surfaces.
* **Digital Decluttering & Object Removal:**
  * Cleanly remove unwanted objects, power cords, personal items, and outdated tenant fixtures while preserving baseboards, flooring, and wall textures.

## 2. Professional Real Estate Photography Techniques
* **Window Pull (HDR Exposure Fusion):**
  * Perfectly balanced exposure showing both crisp, sunlit interior details and clear, vibrant exterior view through windows (no blown-out white highlights).
* **Wide-Angle Perspective (Without Fisheye Distortion):**
  * Equivalent to $16\text{mm} - 20\text{mm}$ full-frame architectural lens with rectilinear correction ensuring straight vertical walls and columns.
* **Twilight / Blue Hour Conversions:**
  * Transform flat daytime exterior photos into dramatic twilight marketing hero shots with glowing warm interior lights and sunset skies.
